About Me

Ali Akbar Kazemi Lari is a Ph.D. student in Educational Management, an official elementary school teacher, and a Teaching Assistant at Islamic Azad University.

His academic interests focus on educational leadership, quality improvement in education, organizational learning, teacher development, and innovative approaches to teaching and learning.

His research aims to enhance educational systems through evidence-based management and innovation.
