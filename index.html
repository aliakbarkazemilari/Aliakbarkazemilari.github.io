About Me

Aliakbar Kazemilari is a Ph.D. student in Educational Management, an official elementary school teacher, and a Teaching Assistant at Islamic Azad University.

His academic interests include educational leadership, educational quality improvement, teacher development, organizational learning, educational technology, and innovative learning systems.

His research activities focus on designing effective educational models and improving learning environments through evidence-based educational management.
