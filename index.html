Introduction

About Me

Ali Akbar Kazemi Lari is an official elementary school teacher, a Ph.D. candidate in Educational Management at Islamic Azad University (Bandar Abbas branch), and a teaching assistant at Islamic Azad University.

His research areas include educational leadership, educational management, improving the quality of educational systems, organizational learning, professional development of teachers, educational technology, and promoting a culture of peace.

His research activities aim to develop effective educational systems and apply evidence-based approaches in management and learning.
