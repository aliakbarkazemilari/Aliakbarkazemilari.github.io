About Me

Aliakbar Kazemilari (Kazemilari) is a Ph.D. student in Educational Management, an official elementary school teacher, and a Teaching Assistant at Islamic Azad University.

His academic interests focus on educational leadership, quality improvement in education, organizational learning, teacher development, and innovative approaches to teaching and learning.

His research activities aim to contribute to the development of effective educational systems through evidence-based management, educational innovation, and future-oriented learning approaches.
