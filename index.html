About Me

Ali Akbar Kazemi Lari (Kazemilari) is a Ph.D. student in Educational Management, an official elementary school teacher, and a Teaching Assistant at Islamic Azad University.

His academic interests focus on educational leadership, quality improvement in education, organizational learning, teacher development, and innovative approaches to teaching and learning.

His research activities aim to contribute to effective educational systems through evidence-based management and educational innovation.
